# Vault — Product Data Science Project Brief

## 1. Company

**Vault** is a fictional mobile-first digital banking product offering everyday banking services such as accounts, cards, transfers, and payments.

## 2. Stakeholder

**Head of Product**

## 3. Business Problem

Vault is acquiring new users, but Product lacks a clear understanding of whether those users are becoming meaningfully activated and engaged customers.

The Head of Product has asked Data Science to investigate customer behaviour, identify the behaviours associated with sustained engagement, and determine how those insights could be used to improve activation among newly acquired users.

## 4. Primary Business Question

> **What behaviours characterize engaged customers, and how can those behaviours help us activate more newly acquired users?**

## 5. Success Criteria

The project will be successful if Product can:

- Establish a defensible, data-driven definition of an **engaged customer**.
- Understand which early customer behaviours are associated with sustained engagement.
- Identify newly acquired users who are more or less likely to become engaged using early behavioural signals.
- Translate the findings into an actionable product intervention or hypothesis.
- Define how that intervention could be validated experimentally before broader rollout.

## 6. Analytical Mandate

The methodology will be driven by the business question and the evidence available in the data rather than by a predetermined modeling technique.

The investigation may involve descriptive and behavioral analytics, statistical inference, cohort and retention analysis, segmentation, predictive modeling, experimentation, or causal methods where appropriate.

The objective is not to apply machine learning for its own sake, but to use the simplest rigorous methods capable of producing reliable insights and better product decisions.

## Guiding Principle

The project will follow a decision-oriented Product Data Science workflow:

**Business problem → behavioral understanding → analytical evidence → prediction → product hypothesis → experimentation → decision**

# Vault: Product Data Science for Customer Activation & Engagement

## Project Overview

**Vault** is a fictional **mobile-first digital banking product**. This project analyzes **user-level behavioral data**, including transactions and other product interactions, to understand how customers become activated and sustainably engaged. The goal is to **translate these behavioral patterns into evidence-based product actions** that can improve activation and engagement among newly acquired users.

## Business Problem

Vault has seen an increase in new user acquisition, but the Head of Product lacks a clear understanding of whether these users are becoming meaningfully activated and sustainably engaged with the product. The challenge is to identify what meaningful engagement looks like, which early behaviors are associated with it, and where Product can intervene.

## Objectives & Key Questions

The primary question guiding this project is:

_What behaviors characterize engaged customers, and how can those behaviors help us activate more newly acquired users?_
